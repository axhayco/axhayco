## About

I'm a **CSE student who can't seem to leave an idea alone.**

If something makes me curious, I usually end up trying to build it — whether that's a student housing marketplace, a developer tool, an AI-powered application, a weird storytelling experiment, or a website for a real business.

I started with **"I wonder if I can build this."**

Somewhere along the way, that became:

**idea → prototype → break it → fix it → deploy it → repeat.**

I enjoy the entire process — figuring out the UI, wiring the backend, fighting bugs at 2 AM, learning whatever technology the idea suddenly demands, and eventually watching something I imagined actually work.

I'm especially interested in the intersection of **software, AI, automation, and product thinking**.

Right now, I'm learning by building — and building a lot.

- 🔭 Currently building: **Hozztl** — making student housing discovery less painful
- 🧠 Exploring: **AI agents, automation & practical AI systems**
- 🛠️ Building with: **React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, Supabase & whatever the next idea needs**
- 🚀 Side quest: **building products instead of just collecting tutorials**
- 🎯 Long-term: **become the kind of engineer who can take an idea from a blank screen to something people actually use**

## 🧠 What I'm Into

I don't really have a single lane yet.

And honestly, I like it that way.

**AI** — not just prompting models, but figuring out what can actually be built around them.

**Products** — because a working app is more interesting to me than another tutorial project.

**Automation** — if a repetitive process can be turned into a system, I'm probably going to try.

**Web development** — still my home ground. I like taking something from a blank screen all the way to deployment.

**Startups & ideas** — I'm constantly collecting them. The difficult part is deciding which ones deserve to be built.
